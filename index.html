The 90-Day Plan

ONE SYSTEM

Right now, getting found on Google, getting booked, and seeing what’s working all live in different places. For 90 days we put them in one system you own, including your T-Shape 2 marketing.

What we build

We will build and run one system across Google, content, video, automation, client data, and reporting.

When

Days 1–30: Fix and build. Days 31–60: Run and improve. Days 61–90: Measure, document, and hand over.

By the end of week one: your Google profile cleanup is under way, and you’ll have your Day 0 map scan, a before picture to compare against on Day 90.

MAXX Circle™

Give more where it can matter most.

MAXX Circle™ is a founder-led philosophy at MACS Digital Media. For selected clients, Stacy personally approves additional work when we believe something beyond the original agreement can genuinely help the business keep moving forward.

The idea is simple

Our Delivery Guarantee

We stand behind our work.

If the written 90-day deliverables are not completed, the final installment is not due, and MACS keeps working for up to 30 additional days at no professional-service charge.

What the Guarantee Covers

Work MACS controls. Delays caused by missing access, unsigned required agreements, unavailable participants, or approvals not given within 7 days of MACS sending them for review move the affected delivery date.
