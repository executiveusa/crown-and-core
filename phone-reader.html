What We Need From You

Five things needed to continue.

Account Access

Access to the accounts required for the agreed work.

Accurate Business Information

Current services, pricing, product details, event-space pricing, provider information, and anything that is off-limits or sensitive. We have already discussed most of this, and we will only need what we can’t find on Square or online. We will proactively handle the rest and won’t bother you for information we can find ourselves.

Approvals

Timely approval of content, health wording, review replies, and campaign plans.

Why? We do not publish sensitive or health-related material without required approval.

Filming Participation

Confirmed dates, team members, participating clients, services, and releases.

Data Permission

Required agreements before client-level Square data is accessed.

Why? Client information must be handled with clear permission and boundaries. We will also comply with applicable Washington laws governing client and health-related information. Please share this contract with your attorney if you have any questions about data safety and permissions.

What We Will Not Do

How we protect Crown & Core

No Unapproved Advertising

Why? You control advertising spend and campaign approval.

No Unsupported Health Claims

Why? Health information must remain accurate, approved, and within allowed claims.

No Client Data Without Permission

Why? Marketing work does not require unrestricted access to clinical information.

No Fake Numbers or Rankings

Why? Every reported number should trace to a source. If we cannot verify it, we do not present it as fact.

No Unapproved Client Content

Why? Client stories, images, video, and before/after material require proper permission before use.
